In 2082, sixty-five thousand probes burn up in the atmosphere all at once – something has photographed the Earth. The trail leads to a comet far out past the Kuiper belt, and the ship *Theseus* is sent to answer it. Her crew is a deliberately inhuman one: a linguist running four personalities in one skull, a biologist wired more to his instruments than his own senses, a soldier, an observer who long ago stopped being able to feel anything he watches – and a resurrected vampire in command.

## Review

I'm not going to lie, that vampire thing really threw me off the first half of the book. It felt like the true alien in this arche-typical humanity meets aliens story, but towards the second half I understood why it was essential for the plot twist. If you enjoy a well-written sci-fi story about the decoupling of intelligence and consciousness, this book might be just the thing for you.
